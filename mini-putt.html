System Brief: Mini-putt

Mini-putt loads the moment you jack in — no client to install, no queue, just a live session in your browser tab.

Why It Hits

Mini-putt keeps the learning curve short on purpose, but leaves enough depth in later stages that getting better still feels earned.

Pro Move

Run Mini-putt in short back-to-back sessions rather than one long grind — pattern recognition compounds faster with repetition than with raw playtime.

FAQ

Is Mini-putt free to run?
Yes — Mini-putt runs in-browser at no cost and doesn't require an account.

Do I need to download a client for Mini-putt?
No. Mini-putt executes entirely inside the page, same as every other title in this grid.

Will Mini-putt run on a locked-down machine?
Being lightweight and browser-based, Mini-putt typically runs fine on school or work machines, though local network rules can still apply.
