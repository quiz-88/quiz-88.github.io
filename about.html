About

quiz-88 is a browser game grid built on one rule: click a tile, be in the session within a second or two. No accounts, no client — everything runs inside the page.

The library spans racing, shooting, puzzle, arcade, clicker, and multiplayer titles. New entries get added over time, and visitors can suggest games through the Contact page.

quiz-88 doesn't develop the games it lists. Each title remains the property of its original creator or publisher; quiz-88 provides a browser-based grid and embed for fast access.

quiz-88 writes its own game write-ups — they are not supplied or reviewed by the game publishers.
